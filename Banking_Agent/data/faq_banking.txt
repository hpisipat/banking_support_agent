BANKING SUPPORT FAQ — KNOWLEDGE BASE
=====================================

SECTION 1: ACCOUNT RELATED
===========================

Q: What is the minimum balance for a savings account?
A: The minimum balance for a savings account is Rs.1,000 for urban 
branches and Rs.500 for rural branches. Failure to maintain this 
balance attracts a penalty of Rs.100 per month for urban branches 
and Rs.50 per month for rural branches.

Q: What happens if my account becomes dormant?
A: An account is classified as dormant if there are no customer 
initiated transactions for 24 months. To reactivate a dormant 
account, visit your nearest branch with original KYC documents 
and submit a written reactivation request. The account will be 
reactivated within 1 working day.

Q: How do I close my bank account?
A: To close your account, visit your home branch with your 
passbook, debit card, cheque book, and a written closure request. 
Ensure your account balance is zero or withdraw remaining funds. 
Processing takes 3-5 working days.

Q: What are the charges for non-maintenance of minimum balance?
A: Non-maintenance charges are Rs.100 per month for urban branches 
and Rs.50 per month for rural branches. These are auto-debited 
on the last day of the month.

Q: Can I have a joint account?
A: Yes, you can open a joint account with up to 3 account holders. 
All holders must provide KYC documents. The account can be operated 
as Either or Survivor or Jointly as per mandate selected.

Q: What is the maximum cash deposit allowed per day?
A: Cash deposits up to Rs.2,00,000 per day are allowed without 
any documentation. Deposits above Rs.2,00,000 require PAN card 
and source of funds declaration.


SECTION 2: LOANS AND INTEREST RATES
=====================================

Q: What is the current interest rate for personal loan?
A: Personal loan interest rates range from 10.5% to 18% per annum 
depending on your credit profile, employment type, and loan tenure. 
Salaried employees with CIBIL score above 750 qualify for the 
lowest rate of 10.5%.

Q: What is the interest rate for home loan?
A: Home loan interest rates start from 8.5% per annum for salaried 
customers with CIBIL score above 750. Rates go up to 11% for 
self-employed applicants. Rates are linked to repo rate and subject 
to change.

Q: What is the interest rate for car loan?
A: Car loan interest rates range from 9% to 13% per annum. New car 
loans start at 9% and used car loans start at 11%. Rate depends on 
vehicle age, loan amount, and applicant profile.

Q: What is the processing fee for loans?
A: Processing fees are as follows:
- Personal Loan: 1% to 2% of loan amount, minimum Rs.1,000
- Home Loan: 0.5% of loan amount, maximum Rs.10,000
- Car Loan: 0.5% of loan amount, minimum Rs.2,500
Processing fees are non-refundable once application is processed.

Q: What are the prepayment charges for loans?
A: Prepayment charges are:
- Personal Loan: 2% of outstanding principal if prepaid before 
  12 months. No charges after 12 months.
- Home Loan: No prepayment charges for floating rate loans.
- Car Loan: 2% of outstanding principal at time of prepayment.

Q: What is the maximum loan tenure?
A: Maximum loan tenures are:
- Personal Loan: 5 years
- Home Loan: 30 years
- Car Loan: 7 years
- Education Loan: 15 years


SECTION 3: CREDIT AND DEBIT CARDS
====================================

Q: What is the annual fee for credit card?
A: Annual fees depend on the card variant:
- Classic Card: Rs.500 per year, waived on annual spend of Rs.50,000
- Gold Card: Rs.1,000 per year, waived on annual spend of Rs.1,00,000
- Platinum Card: Rs.2,500 per year, waived on annual spend of Rs.2,50,000

Q: What is the interest rate on credit card outstanding?
A: Interest on unpaid credit card outstanding is charged at 3.5% 
per month (42% per annum). To avoid interest, pay the full 
outstanding amount by the due date every month.

Q: What are the reward points on credit card?
A: Reward points are earned as follows:
- 1 point per Rs.100 spent on regular purchases
- 2 points per Rs.100 spent on grocery and dining
- 5 points per Rs.100 spent on partner merchants
1 reward point = Rs.0.25 value. Points expire after 2 years.

Q: What is the daily transaction limit for debit card?
A: Default daily limits are:
- ATM cash withdrawal: Rs.25,000 per day
- POS transactions: Rs.1,00,000 per day
- Online transactions: Rs.50,000 per day
Limits can be customised via net banking or mobile app.

Q: What are cash withdrawal charges on credit card?
A: Cash withdrawal on credit card attracts:
- Fee: 2.5% of amount withdrawn, minimum Rs.500
- Interest: 3.5% per month from date of withdrawal
- No interest-free period for cash withdrawals


SECTION 4: DIGITAL BANKING
============================

Q: What are NEFT transaction charges and timings?
A: NEFT is available 24x7 including weekends and holidays.
Charges are:
- Up to Rs.10,000: Rs.2.50
- Rs.10,001 to Rs.1,00,000: Rs.5
- Rs.1,00,001 to Rs.2,00,000: Rs.15
- Above Rs.2,00,000: Rs.25

Q: What are RTGS transaction charges and timings?
A: RTGS is available 24x7. Minimum amount is Rs.2,00,000.
Charges are:
- Rs.2,00,000 to Rs.5,00,000: Rs.25
- Above Rs.5,00,000: Rs.50

Q: What is the UPI transaction limit?
A: UPI transaction limits are:
- Per transaction: Rs.1,00,000
- Per day: Rs.1,00,000 (across all UPI apps)
- Exception: Rs.2,00,000 for specific categories like 
  healthcare and education

Q: What are IMPS charges?
A: IMPS is available 24x7. Charges are:
- Up to Rs.1,000: Rs.2.50
- Rs.1,001 to Rs.1,00,000: Rs.5
- Rs.1,00,001 to Rs.2,00,000: Rs.15
- Above Rs.2,00,000: Rs.25


SECTION 5: KYC AND COMPLIANCE
================================

Q: What documents are required for KYC?
A: For KYC verification you need:
- Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, or Voter ID
- Address proof: Aadhaar card, Utility bill (not older than 3 months),
  Passport, or Driving licence
- Recent passport size photograph
- PAN card is mandatory for all accounts

Q: When does KYC expire and how to renew?
A: KYC is valid for:
- Low risk customers: 10 years
- Medium risk customers: 8 years  
- High risk customers: 2 years
To renew, visit your branch with updated KYC documents or 
use the bank's Video KYC facility available on the mobile app.

Q: What is the nomination process?
A: Nomination can be added at account opening or later. 
To add or update nomination, submit Form DA1 at your branch 
or update via net banking under Account Settings. 
Only one nominee is allowed per account.

Q: What is the process for Aadhaar linking?
A: Link Aadhaar to your bank account via:
1. Net banking: Login → My Account → Link Aadhaar
2. Mobile app: Profile → Link Aadhaar
3. ATM: Select Aadhaar Seeding option
4. Branch: Submit Aadhaar copy with account details
Linking is mandatory for government subsidy accounts.


SECTION 6: FIXED DEPOSITS
===========================

Q: What are the current FD interest rates?
A: Fixed deposit interest rates are:
- 7 days to 29 days: 4.00% per annum
- 30 days to 90 days: 5.50% per annum
- 91 days to 180 days: 6.00% per annum
- 181 days to 364 days: 6.50% per annum
- 1 year to 2 years: 7.50% per annum
- 2 years to 5 years: 7.00% per annum
- Above 5 years: 6.75% per annum
Senior citizens get 0.50% additional interest on all tenures.

Q: What is the penalty for premature FD withdrawal?
A: Premature withdrawal penalty is 1% on the applicable rate.
Example: If FD was booked at 7.5% and withdrawn early,
interest paid will be at 6.5%. Minimum lock-in period is 7 days.

Q: What is the minimum and maximum FD amount?
A: Minimum FD amount is Rs.1,000. There is no maximum limit.
Tax Saver FD minimum is Rs.1,000 and maximum is Rs.1,50,000 
per financial year with 5 year lock-in period.
